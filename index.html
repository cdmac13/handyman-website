Answering Your Questions

Frequently Asked Technical Questions

Transparent, honest, and direct. Here is what you should know about hiring us in Louisville, KY.

Do you require a general contractor or trade-specific plumbing license? +

In Louisville and the state of Kentucky, general contractor or specific mechanical licenses (like plumbing or electrical) are strictly required for structural changes, main utility alterations, or structural additions.

Eric the Handyman & Remodeling focuses strictly on non-structural, cosmetic home improvement and finish carpentry. This includes custom tile shower construction (connecting to existing rough-in pipe drains), bathroom vanity replacements (using existing shutoff valves), drywall repair, trim installation, and painting. By avoiding structural changes, we operate in 100% compliance with local building codes, saving you expensive permit overheads and delays.

Why do you focus on week-long projects? +

Quality tile work and remodeling require sequential preparation and curing phases (such as waterproofing membranes drying, thinset curing, grouting, and sealing).

By targeting five-day, week-long blocks, we can commit 100% of our daily attention to one client. Eric stays on your job from demolition to final cleanup, maintaining high velocity and absolute quality control, without jumping between projects.

What waterproofing systems do you utilize for tile showers? +

We do not cut corners with cheap plastic liners or cement board alone. We build using industry-certified Schluter-KERDI waterproofing systems. This includes fully bonded waterproof membranes, pre-sloped shower pans, waterproof band seals on all seams, and specialized corner seal components. This ensures a 100% mold-proof, water-tight shower that stands up to decades of daily use.

How does the interactive project estimator calculate pricing? +

Our estimator is built on current regional material costs and competitive labor rates for the Greater Kentuckiana area. By providing this tool, we aim to offer complete price transparency from the very first click.

When you input your project size and select a material quality level, the tool calculates a realistic ballpark range for both labor and materials. This ensures our quotes remain fair and market-accurate, reflecting the high-fidelity craftsmanship we bring to every dedicated project week.
