Frequently Asked Questions

Transparent, honest, and direct. Here is what you should know about hiring us in Louisville, KY.

What areas in Louisville do you serve? +

We proudly serve homeowners and businesses throughout Louisville and the Greater Kentuckiana area. As a locally owned small business, we are deeply committed to elevating the homes in our community.

Do you take on general handyman requests or custom projects? +

Absolutely. While we specialize in premium tile installations and bathroom remodels, Eric's 35+ years of experience covers a vast array of home repair and maintenance needs. If you have a custom request, just mention it in the consultation form!

What waterproofing systems do you utilize for tile showers? +

We do not cut corners. We build using industry-certified Schluter-KERDI waterproofing systems. This includes fully bonded waterproof membranes, pre-sloped shower pans, and specialized corner seals. This ensures a 100% mold-proof, water-tight shower that stands up to decades of daily use.
